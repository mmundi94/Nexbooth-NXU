# Nexbooth

**The Nexford photo booth.** Face lenses that follow you like Snapchat or TikTok filters, the Nexford alpaca, frames, filters, and a photo strip mode, with themes for every season.

- **4 themes:** Everyday, Halloween, Thanksgiving, and Holidays. The booth picks the right one by date (October is Halloween, November is Thanksgiving, December is Holidays), and anyone can switch at the top.
- **Face lenses:** 24 lenses that track up to 4 faces at once. Some have a surprise when you open your mouth (confetti, candy, bats, leaves, snow, hearts).
- **The alpaca:** 16 cute alpaca poses to drag into photos, plus themed stickers and speech bubbles.
- **Photos:** frames, color filters, falling snow, leaves, bats and confetti, a countdown timer, "Strip of 4", download, and share on phones.
- **Private:** everything runs in the browser. No photos or face data are uploaded or stored anywhere.

## Put it online with GitHub Pages (about 10 minutes)

### 1. Create the repository

1. Sign in at [github.com](https://github.com).
2. Click **+** at the top right, then **New repository**.
3. Name it (for example `nexbooth`), choose **Public**, and click **Create repository**.

### 2. Upload the files

1. Unzip `nexbooth.zip`. Open the **nexbooth** folder.
2. In your new repository, click **uploading an existing file** (or **Add file > Upload files**).
3. Select **everything inside** the nexbooth folder (`index.html`, `README.md`, and the `assets`, `css`, `js`, `tools`, and `vendor` folders) and drag it into GitHub.
   - `index.html` must be at the top level, not inside another folder.
   - The `vendor` folder holds the face tracking files (about 17 MB). Wait until every file finishes uploading.
4. Click **Commit changes**.

### 3. Turn on GitHub Pages

1. Click **Settings**, then **Pages** in the left menu.
2. Under **Build and deployment**, set **Source** to **Deploy from a branch**.
3. Choose the **main** branch and the **/ (root)** folder, then click **Save**.
4. Wait 1 to 3 minutes and refresh. Your link appears at the top: `https://YOUR-USERNAME.github.io/nexbooth/`

## Tips for the team

- Pick a **face lens** from the row under the camera. It finds your face by itself.
- Open your mouth on lenses that say so for a surprise.
- Add the **alpaca** from the panel. Drag to move. Drag the round corner handle to resize and rotate. On a phone, pinch.
- Press the big button or the **space bar** to take a photo.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | Fix |
|---|---|
| The page shows "404" | Make sure `index.html` is at the top level of the repository. Wait two more minutes and refresh. |
| "Camera access was blocked" | Click the camera icon in the address bar, choose **Allow**, and reload. |
| "Your camera is busy" | Close Zoom, Teams, or other apps using the camera. |
| Lenses say "not available here" | Lenses need the online link (they do not work when you open `index.html` straight from your computer). Also make sure the whole `vendor` folder was uploaded. |
| Lenses feel slow | Face tracking runs on the device. Older laptops may be a little slower. Good light helps. |
| Fonts look plain | The fonts come from Google Fonts. Some networks block them. Everything still works. |

## Changing things

- **Lenses, phrases, captions, and theme defaults:** edit `js/themes.js`. It is a simple list you can change on GitHub (open the file, click the pencil, edit, and commit).
- **Artwork:** all alpaca, sticker, and lens drawings are generated by `tools/art/build.py` into `js/assets.js`.
